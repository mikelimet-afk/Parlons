# Design: sports and "how long"

Status: approved and built

## Why

The user wants to talk about sports in French: whether you play, which sport, and how long you've been playing. That "how long" sentence needs *depuis*, which the course didn't teach.

## What gets built

1. **Lesson "Sports & how long"**, at the end of the A2 chapter "Telling stories".
   - About 25 words: sports, equipment, places, *une équipe*, *un match*, *s'entraîner*, *gagner*, *perdre*.
   - Grammar: *jouer au foot* versus *faire du vélo / de la natation*; *depuis* + present, *il y a* + past, *ça fait… que*.
   - Questions: *Tu fais du sport ?*, *Depuis combien de temps tu joues ?*
2. **A rule page** for *depuis / il y a / ça fait*, linked from the lesson.
3. **Two Everyday French conversations**: chatting about sports with a new friend (tu), and signing up at a sports club (vous).
4. **Drawings** for about 20 of the new sports nouns.
5. **Dictionary**: every new word in the dictionary under the lesson, with an example sentence.

## Approaches

- A sports theme of words only. Fast, but it can't answer "how long have you played".
- **A lesson with grammar, a rule page and conversations (chosen).** It teaches the sentence the user asked for and fits the course's existing format.

## Not in this part

The slowly appearing rule explanation. It's designed together with the B1 and B2 lessons.

## Builder's calls

- Placement at the end of chapter 4 (A2), since *depuis* + present comes before B1 tenses.
- Words already in the dictionary keep their entries and are added to the lesson through `FR.LESSON_MORE`.
- All sentences are original, with neutral names.
